# Code Of Conduct

Be respectful, specific and evidence-led. Critique code and claims rather than
people. Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, deliberate disclosure of
private information and hostile disruption are not accepted.

Security-sensitive reports should follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) rather than
publishing game binaries, credentials, private logs or exploit details in a
public issue. Maintainers may edit, remove or reject contributions and restrict
participation when necessary to protect contributors and users.
