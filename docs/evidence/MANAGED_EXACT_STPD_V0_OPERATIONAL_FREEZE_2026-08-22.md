## Operational Contract

STPD may consume only a stable Snapshot with a complete finite BoundAction
catalog. Every action binds the current Snapshot and one opaque action ID.
Unknown delivery is never retried. A stale refusal requires a fresh observation
and new selection. Receipt proves delivery; the stable successor or terminal is
separate truth.

An episode/data segment is invalid after incomplete authority, settling
timeout, missing successor, stale replay, unknown delivery, identity drift,
request/action/Receipt mismatch or an unexpected environment exception.

## Deferred Qualification

The freeze does not claim exhaustive/randomized CrossHost equivalence, every
card/relic/event/selector, 72-hour or 10-million-decision soak, broad fault
matrix, real changed-build requalification, high-core/cluster operation,
cross-platform support or policy quality. Any exact identity or hard-shell
change reopens the affected gates.
