# Platform Human Evidence

Use this Skill when a task explicitly involves native-human evidence
preparation, capture, audit, bundle classification, or closeout. It does not
authorize gameplay actions, prove Human origin by machine, or decide research
admission.

## Inputs

Identify the exact loaded game/Modset/runtime and component artifact identities,
capture profile and action families, owner-operated steps, target evidence
level, session location outside Git, audit/pack commands, rollback, and stop
conditions.

## Workflow

1. Read root and Annotator/Evidence/Game Mod guidance, `docs/TESTING.md`, Status,
   and only the exact capture/audit contract and relevant dated evidence.
2. Verify source/test/build/install/load identities independently. A predecessor
   session cannot qualify new bytes.
3. Prepare the runtime and recording boundary, then stop for the Human owner to
   perform native actions and attest origin. Do not simulate Human origin.
4. Preserve accepted, cancelled, invalidated, unknown, unresolved, and
   out-of-scope outcomes. Do not convert absence or incomplete capture into a
   successful transition.
5. Audit with the independent typed tools, preserve immutable raw evidence
   outside Git, and quarantine or fail closed on identity/schema/content drift.
6. Classify exact action-family coverage, Reads, successor semantics,
   non-interference, and non-claims. Verification never grants corpus or
   training admission.

Never commit raw Human data, local sessions/stores, game files, credentials, or
installed artifacts. Never retry an uncertain mutation automatically.

## Output and stop

Return exact identities, owner-attested actions, machine-verifiable audit
results, counts by disposition/family, evidence level, rollback, and non-claims.
Stop for Human action/attestation, unavailable exact runtime, unsafe identity,
authority ambiguity, or any unresolved evidence write.

## Trigger evals

- Positive: “Prepare the exact recorder, pause for my native play, then audit
  and classify the Human session bundle.”
- Negative: “Edit the evidence README and fix its Markdown links.”
- Overlap: Package/load qualification routes to
  `platform-runtime-qualification`; this Skill owns only the Human
  preparation/capture/audit/classification slice.
